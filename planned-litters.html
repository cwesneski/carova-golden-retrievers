UKC CH Carova & Lake Gaston Crazy 4 Fun “Giggles”

 

Giggles is Co Owned with my dear friend Teresa Joyner of Lake Gaston Goldens. Giggles name captures the essence of our many adventures and the endless laughter we’ve enjoyed together over the years. Giggles also shares that beautiful golden temperament balanced in play and cuddles. She is extremely patient with puppies and gets along well with other dogs. Giggles earned her UKC Championship quickly and was working on her Grand Championship before taking a break to be a mom.
